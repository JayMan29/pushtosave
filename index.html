Why it matters

When seconds count, action matters.

Immediate CPR from someone nearby can double or triple a person's chance of survival after cardiac arrest. Learning what to do gives more people the confidence to respond.

About Push to Save

A community that shows up.

Push to Save is a student-led initiative to make lifesaving CPR skills more accessible. We focus on hands-on learning, outreach in multiple languages, and partnerships with local health advocates and first responders.

Our goal is simple: help more people feel prepared to act for a friend, family member, or stranger.
